\begin{column}{0.42\textwidth}
\begin{block}{Search effort is zero, and equal}
\footnotesize
No hyperparameter search was run. Every arm uses the same settings, fixed before training, and
changes \textbf{exactly one} of them.

Every score is therefore a score \emph{under a common protocol at an untuned configuration}, not
the best that configuration could reach.
\end{block}

\begin{alertblock}{Reproducibility limit, reported not hidden}
\footnotesize
The Apple GPU backend has no deterministic kernel for the scatter-add DeepLabV3 reaches when it
interpolates logits back to full resolution. U-Net reproduces bit-exactly; DeepLabV3 diverges by
${\sim}4\times10^{-7}$ per step.
\end{alertblock}

\vspace{-0.1cm}
\begin{block}{Decision rule, fixed before any result was seen}
$A$ beats $B$ \emph{only} when its mean validation mIoU is higher \textbf{and} the two intervals
of mean $\pm$ one standard deviation across three seeds \textbf{do not overlap}.

Overlapping intervals $\Rightarrow$ \textbf{indistinguishable}, which is itself a result and is
\emph{not} resolved by picking the higher mean. A claim about one class applies the same rule to
that class's IoU. One seed supports no claim of superiority.
\end{block}
